Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 11, 2026

At TruthLens, your privacy is our highest priority. We want you to understand how we handle text data submitted for analysis.

1. Text Data Handling

When you submit text manually or through our screen capture overlay, the text is securely transmitted to our backend server where perplexity, burstiness, vocabulary richness, and repetition metrics are evaluated. This processing occurs in memory, and the results are returned instantly. We do NOT save, log, or store your submitted texts on our servers.

2. Local Storage

Your search history is saved strictly on your local device's internal storage. We do not sync this history to the cloud or share it with third parties. You can clear your search history anytime through the settings inside the app.

3. No Personal Data Collection

We do not collect personal identifiers, such as names, email addresses (except when you contact support directly), location data, or device identifiers.

4. Agreement and Policy Changes

By using TruthLens, you consent to the terms of this Privacy Policy. We may update this policy periodically, and changes will be posted on this page.
